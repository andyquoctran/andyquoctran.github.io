# research


{% capture winter_body %}
A question left unanswered from the previous summer was whether or not we can construct a Brill--Noether special loop of loops in every even genus? We are hoping new tools in *Prym--Brill--Noether theory* can help us answer this.
{% endcapture %}

{% capture summer_body %}
In 2012, Cools--Draisma--Payne--Robeva proved a tropical analogue of the Brill--Noether theorem. Since then, tropical Brill--Noether theory has been a fruitful area of study. In fact, Nathan Pflueger gave us a full characterization of the behavor of these loci on chains of loops of generic lengths.
{: .muted}
Consider the graph shown here. The genus $3$ and $4$ graphs are well known examples to relevant algebraic properties. Overall these graphs have been only novelly explored. Together with my advisor, Prof. Montserrat Texidor, we give a condition for hyperellipticity on all metric realizations of the loop of loops of genus $g$, and make steps toward showing the existence of a Brill--Noether special metric realization of the graph for any genus.
{: .muted}
